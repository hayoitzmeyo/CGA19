About Us

Mission Statement

Our goal is to empower every homeowner and community with clear, accurate, and actionable risk insights—using the power of advanced technology and data—so that everyone can protect what matters most and confidently face the challenges of tomorrow.

Our Story

Every few months, another headline reminds us of how unpredictable our world has become. A 5.2 earthquake shook Southern California in April 2025. Catastrophic floods swept through Texas that July, submerging towns in minutes. In August, a landslide-triggered tsunami hit Alaska's fjords.

Each event was different, but the same question lingered: Were we really prepared?

We realized that millions of people make one of the biggest decisions of their lives, where to live, without ever truly knowing the risks and how to solve or reduce these risks. Flood maps are outdated, earthquake forecasts are buried in research papers, and wildfire data often sits behind paywalls, making it difficult for everyday families to access and interpret effectively.

We built Georisk to change that. Born from our community’s own experience with disaster, it empowers people everywhere to understand their local risks clearly, before tragedy strikes. Our goal is simple: to turn uncertainty into knowledge, and knowledge into safety, so no one is ever left in the dark again.

Natural disasters might seem "random" at first can become predictable and solvable with the help of data science and technology. The data exists, but it’s scattered, complex, and often locked behind paywalls or technical jargon. Families, renters, and even real estate professionals are left in the dark, unable to make truly informed decisions about their safety and future.

Determined to change this, our team came together, spending months gathering decades of historical data, honing our skills, and developing advanced prediction models capable of accurately determining the risks of not only wildfires, but also floods and earthquakes. However, we went one step further, integrating collected data from different recommendations others with similar risks suggested and outputting it to an individual with a similar risk. Every line of code, every data point, was driven by a simple belief:

Knowledge is power, and everyone deserves it

Today, Georisk empowers households to make safer, smarter choices, before disaster strikes. Our story is not as tragic as those who suffered devastating losses at the hands of seemingly uncontrollable events, but they laid the foundation of a movement to turn uncertainty into empowerment, and to ensure that no one is ever left in the dark again.
